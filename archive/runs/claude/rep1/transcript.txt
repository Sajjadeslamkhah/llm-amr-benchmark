- **ISO_115 ampicillin:** AMRFinderPlus reported blaTEM as only 54% present. A BLAST search showed the whole gene is there at 100% identity, just split across two contigs where the assembly broke. I counted it as intact, so ampicillin is R.
- **ISO_116 meropenem:** this is the least certain call. The isolate has no carbapenemase, but it carries an AmpC enzyme (CMY-42) and a broken outer-membrane porin gene (ompF), which together can raise carbapenem resistance. That mostly affects ertapenem, so I called meropenem S.
- **Trimethoprim/sulfamethoxazole:** I called R only when a trimethoprim-resistance gene (dfrA) was present. A sulfonamide gene alone, as in ISO_117, was called S.
