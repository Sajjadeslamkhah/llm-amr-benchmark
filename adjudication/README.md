## Verdicts as they stand

Four H1 candidates (oqxAB, strB, strA, crpP) were rejected by both readers, and by
reader 1 twice. No reader found a determinant that does not exist in the literature.
That result is stable across readers, across reader 1's two reports, and against the
reader instructions from which these four names had been removed precisely so that
naming them could not give the verdict away.

Both H3 candidates are unresolved, and the reason is a defect in the sheet rather
than a disagreement between readers. Reader 2 returned "Unclear" on both, asking
which mutation one refers to and which strain the other applies to. Reader 1's second
report names the same gap in its limitations: the two threshold rows do not state the
antibiotic or the bacterial species, so the 0.25 mg/L breakpoint could be checked
only against Enterobacterales ciprofloxacin in general and not against the case in
hand. Two readers working separately identified the same missing context.

An H3 row therefore cannot be adjudicated as it is built. The screen reports the
sentence the claim sits in but not the isolate and antibiotic the claim was made
about, and a breakpoint is meaningless without them. Those fields are to be added and
the two rows re-adjudicated by both readers.
